Limited Edition Items at Skyline

Not all Skyline Chili stores are the same, some our owned by the company of Skyline itself, corporate stores. While others are owned by specific people/families, franchises.

All corporate owned Skylines will sell everything on the menu, even some of the special edition items year round. While stores like franchises, the owners of that store can choose whether or not they want to keep something that is special edition.

Mac and Cheese

Once a year, Skyline Chili adds mac and cheese to their menu. The mac and cheese is made with a white cheese sauce.

Our most popular way to order the mac and cheese is in a 3-way. Instead of using spaghetti for a 3-way, replace it with the mac and cheese. However, you can add mac and cheese to any food item, or even have the mac and cheese all by itself.

Skyline Dip Chilito

The Skyline dip chilito combines two fan favorite items, the chilito and Skyline chili dip.

The Skyline dip chilito starts like a regular chilito, with a warm tortilla, however with the dip chilito, you add a layer of cream cheese, then chili and cheese, and finally topping it off with fritos.

Desserts

The Big Scoop

One of the limited time desserts that Skyline offers is our very own ice cream flavor, The Big Scoop.

We partner with Graters Ice Cream for our own Skyline flavored ice cream with similar spices used as the chili, and even oyster crackers throughout the ice cream.

Floats

Throughout the summer time, Skyline offers ice cream floats that can be made with any soda that we have. This is the most popular with children, but it can be enjoyed at any age!
